About this site & how to cite it

What this database is, what it deliberately leaves out, and how to quote from it.

What this is

A sourced public reference documenting every published edition, signed copy, contribution and notable artifact connected to the American novelist Donna Tartt (b. 1963). It is organised in two tiers: Tier A is work by Tartt — the novels and their editions, her short fiction and essays, her introductions and contributions, the audiobooks she narrated, and her on-record interviews. Tier B is material about her that she did not write. The distinction is kept rigidly, because conflating the two is how most misinformation about Tartt starts.

Sources and evidence

Every page on this site is generated from files in the repository: the cover manifest (data/cover_manifest.csv), the master reference, the Tier B list, the interview transcripts, and the captures under assets/. Nothing is written for the website that is not already in the database. Cover images are republished here as downscaled derivatives — each plate links to the full-resolution original in the repository. Captures, PDFs and transcripts are cited by a link to the file itself.

What is not here

One category is held privately: full-book scans of the works Tartt contributed to. Only her own contribution is relevant and that is excerpted into the public repository; the complete copyrighted books are not redistributed. They are cited by publication and ISBN instead.

Claims are cross-checked against primary sources where possible. Documented corrections, and any entries that remain single-sourced or unsettled, are recorded in the verification report — read it before treating a lone entry as settled. Per-edition caveats and points of issue sit in the relevant chapter of the reference.

How to cite

Cite the underlying artifact, not this website, wherever the artifact is named. Each item page carries a ready-made citation with its catalogue ID and reference section; interview pages carry the outlet, interviewer, date and original URL. For the database as a whole:

Donna Tartt — A Collector's Reference (research database), 2026. https://github.com/thefloppytaco/donna-tartt-research

Quotations from Tartt's writing and interviews are her words as printed by the original publisher, reproduced for non-commercial research. Descriptive prose in the reference is the compiler's characterisation, not hers — and quotations from her fiction are her characters' words, not her opinions.

Rights

The original research and compilation are licensed CC BY-NC 4.0. Third-party source captures, cover images and quoted text remain under their owners' copyright. To request removal of anything, open an issue on the repository.

How the site is built

A dependency-light Python generator (scripts/build_site.py with scripts/sitelib/) reads the database and writes static HTML into docs/. There is no framework, no CDN, no webfont and no tracking: the stylesheet and a small progressive- enhancement script are the only assets. Re-run python3 scripts/build_site.py after editing the database to regenerate the site.

Images are served as downscaled derivatives. If one is missing, the page falls back to the full-resolution original in the repository, so a plate is never simply broken — it just loads more slowly until the derivatives are rebuilt.
