What is reproduced here. The books Tartt contributed to are other people's copyrighted work, so only her own contribution is extracted into this archive — the foreword, the essay, the translation, the poem. The complete books are held in a separate private archive and are cited here by publication and ISBN. See ARCHITECTURE.md.
