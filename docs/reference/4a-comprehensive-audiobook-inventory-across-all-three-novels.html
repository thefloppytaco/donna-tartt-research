Tartt-narrated record (combining all confirmed entries): she narrates the abridged 2002 Little Friend (Random House Audio); the 2023 unabridged Secret History reissue (Random House Audio); extracts of the 1993 Secret History cassette; the 2006 Recorded Books True Grit (Charles Portis); and at least one story in the multi-voice Winesburg, Ohio (HarperAudio). The 2006 True Grit audiobook also contains her essay on the novel as an afterword.

Format note: original Random House Audio CDs of The Little Friend (2002, abridged, narrated by Tartt) are the only Tartt-narrated commercial recording of one of her own complete novels. The Hachette Audio CD set of The Goldfinch comprises 19 CDs in a publisher box.
